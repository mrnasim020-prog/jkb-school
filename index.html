About Our Institution

Shaping Bright Minds at N.A.S. Vidyapeeth

Established in 2026 at Notun Jaladipur, Basudebpur, Shamsherganj, Murshidabad (W.B.), N.A.S. Vidyapeeth is dedicated to fostering academic excellence, moral integrity, and personal growth among students.

Under the expert guidance of Director Nasim Aktar Sk, our institution provides rigorous academic training paired with co-curricular activities, ensuring children grow into capable and responsible leaders of tomorrow.

Our Mission

Knowledge, Discipline, and Better Tomorrow for every child.

Our Vision

To be the premier center of learning with affordable quality education.

Key Features of N.A.S. Vidyapeeth

  • Bengali & CBSE Curriculum support
  • Regular student evaluation & parent interaction
  • Library & reading-friendly environment
  • Computer & digital learning facilities
  • Moral education & personality development
