From the Principal's Desk

Welcome to N.A.S Vidyapeeth. Our institution stands committed to academic excellence, social values, and the holistic development of our students. We strive to provide a disciplined yet nurturing environment where knowledge transforms into wisdom.

History of the Vidyapeeth

Established in 2026 at Notun Jaladipur, Basudebpur (Murshidabad), N.A.S Vidyapeeth was founded with the aim to impart quality modern higher education to students of all backgrounds and nurture future leaders of our society.

VISION

To cultivate intellectual growth, ethical values, and social responsibility among students through high-quality education.

MISSION

To foster inclusive and innovative learning environments that enable every student to succeed in their career and contribute meaningfully to society.
