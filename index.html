Rules & Disciplines

Student Rules

  • Arrive at least 10 minutes before Assembly.
  • Wear clean, ironed school uniform and ID Card.
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited.
  • Maintain strict discipline in classrooms and corridors.

Staff Regulations

  • Mandatory biometric attendance before assembly.
  • Saree dress code for female teaching staff.
  • Weekly Lesson Plans must be submitted in advance.
  • Corporal punishment is strictly forbidden.
