About N.A.S Vidyapeeth

N.A.S Vidyapeeth is a premier educational institution committed to imparting top-class coaching and comprehensive learning frameworks. Located in Basudebpur, Matun Jaladipur, West Bengal, our mission is to empower students from Nursery to Class XII as well as competitive examination aspirants with conceptual clarity, robust academic rigor, and moral integrity.

Academic & Professional Courses

Explore our wide range of structured programs designed to build academic excellence and competitive readiness.


  • Foundation Classes (Nursery to Class V): Building core basics in arithmetic, language, and environmental awareness.
  • Middle School Curriculum (Class VI to VIII): Enhancing analytical skills across science, mathematics, and humanities.
  • Secondary & Higher Secondary Coaching (Class IX to XII - WBBSE / CBSE): Comprehensive syllabus mastery, board exam strategies, and practical lab guidance.
  • Competitive Examination Wing: Specialized modules for WBCS, SSC, banking, railway, and police recruitment tests.
  • Entrance Coaching Wing: Specialized foundational training for engineering (JEE), medical (NEET), nursing (ANM/GNM), and polytechnic entrance tests.
