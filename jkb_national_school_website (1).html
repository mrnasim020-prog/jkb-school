Code of Conduct & Discipline Guidelines

Note: Full detailed 30-point Student & Parent Codes of Conduct are published in English below. The official policy document is also available at the admin office.

Key Student Rules (30 Points Summary)

  • 1. Arrive at school at least 10 minutes before assembly time.
  • 2. Wear clean, properly ironed prescribed uniform every day.
  • 3. Identity Card must be displayed at all times on school premises.
  • 4. Mobile phones, smartwatches & electronic gadgets are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
  • 5. Respect all teachers, administrative staff, and fellow students.
  • 6. Physical/Mental bullying or abusive language results in strict disciplinary action.
  • 7. Complete homework and daily tasks diligently.
  • 8. Take care of school property; breaking equipment will attract fines.

Parent & Guardian Guidelines

  • 1. Inspect the School Diary daily and sign teacher notices promptly.
  • 2. Mandatory attendance in Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM).
  • 3. Direct entry into classrooms during school hours is strictly prohibited.
  • 4. Schedule prior appointments to meet subject teachers.
  • 5. Pay all school monthly fees within prescribed deadlines.
  • 6. Do not send sick children (contagious illness) until fully recovered.
  • 7. Ensure timely pickup and drop-off at the school gate.
  • 8. Maintain polite and respectful decorum with school management.
